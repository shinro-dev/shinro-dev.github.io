02 — The vocabulary

Three ideas the notes keep using.

You don't need the whole product to follow a demo. These are the three pieces both notes point at, and the words they use for them.

01 · THE ARTIFACT

A kernel, not a runtime

A trained policy is traced, lowered to a typed graph, and emitted as one shared object. Memory is sized at compile time and constants are folded in; there is no interpreter in the loop.

policy.onnx → one .so
02 · THE RESIDENT

A resident that admits

Cake holds signed capsules to declared, versioned contracts, resolves them into a hashed Plan, and reads back the session, slot and generation the console shows.

capsule → contract → hashed plan
03 · THE TRANSACTION

A swap with a beginning and an end

replace-slot computes the dependent closure, drains the retiring generation, rebinds under an epoch guard, publishes the new binding, and reclaims the old. The session never restarts.

closure → drain → rebind → activate
